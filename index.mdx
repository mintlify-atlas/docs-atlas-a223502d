---

PRMS (Planning, Reporting & Management System) is the unified web platform for the OneCGIAR portfolio. It gives Initiative and Center staff a structured way to capture typed research results, align them to Theory of Change outcomes, collect evidence, and move them through a quality assurance workflow before submission — all within a single reporting cycle.

## Core capabilities

<Steps>
  <Step title="Capture typed results">
    Create results across all CGIAR result types — knowledge products, capacity sharing, innovation development and use, IPSR innovation packages, policy change, and others — each with a structured set of common and type-specific fields.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Align to Theory of Change">
    Link each result to one or more Theory of Change outcomes sourced from external ToC services, providing traceability from output to impact pathway.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Quality assurance review">
    Structured QA workflow moves results from **Editing** → **Quality Assessed** → **Submitted**. Reviewers can comment on individual fields; submitters resolve feedback and re-submit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reporting and export">
    Consolidated results feed into Type-One Reports, IPSR pathway views, bilateral export payloads, and platform-level reporting surfaces consumed by downstream BI tools and funders.
  </Step>
</Steps>
